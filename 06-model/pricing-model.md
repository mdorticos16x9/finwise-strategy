# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

**Stage:** Stage 1 · Value Creation, 98% of trials (6,296 of 6,424) never convert, and trial-to-paid stayed between 1.87% and 2.08% every month while modeling usage swung from 11% to 58%. Value isn't landing reliably yet, so pricing is premature.

**Model:** Subscription, because FinWise delivers value continuously (the weekly forecast and the accountant's shared view), so a subscription fits, with the reverse trial kept as the way in. The trade-off: subscription revenue only compounds if users stay, and with 60% leaving within a year, retention has to be fixed before the price is touched. A permanent free tier would add more users who never reach value, and transaction or advertising pricing doesn't match how a finance tool earns trust.

_____

## Recommendation

Don't change the price yet. Move the paywall to where value lands: when the reverse trial ends, the account goes read-only instead of staying usable for free. Owners keep their data and their last forecast, but the 30-day forecast stops updating, the weekly forecast-confidence check pauses, and the shared accountant view locks until they subscribe. Applies to new trials, rolled out by sign-up cohort.

**The pricing bet:** Packaging matched to value: validating that the paid line sits at the live forecast and the accountant view, where owners feel they need more, not at a feature count.

_____

## Model signal

It targets Leading 1 (% of new trials reaching a first forecast in week 1): the more owners reach the forecast during the trial, the more of them feel the loss when it stops updating. If the line is in the right place, upgrades should cluster at trial end among owners who reached the forecast, and stay low among those who never did.
