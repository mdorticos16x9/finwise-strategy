## The data pattern

The headline metric is trial-to-paid, flat at 2%, but the real pattern behind it is a Ceiling: modeling usage swung from 11% to 58% and conversion didn't move: it's pinned at about 2%, roughly 10 paid trials a month, so no usage metric truly moves it, and its 1.87–2.08% wiggle is rounding, not signal. More feature adoption alone won't break it. Users now visit more often for shorter sessions, so the next experiment has to change the experience itself: the weekly forecast check and the accountant invite, not more onboarding tweaks.

_Metric patterns: Conversion, Ceiling: it holds between 1.87% and 2.08% every month, whatever happens to traffic or feature usage. The current experience has taken users as far as it can. Modeling usage, Cliff (Oct 2024): after climbing from 11% to 58%, it drops to 36% in the last month. A sudden drop at one point, so check what changed that month first. Import usage, Ceiling: after a dip in Jan–Mar 2024 it recovers and flattens at about 41–45%, so more than half of trial users never import. Session duration, Cliff (Jul 2024): it falls from 12.1 to 7.0 minutes in one month and stays at 5–9 minutes, in the same months modeling usage rose. Sessions/user, Doesn't fit cleanly: a slow leak from 9 to 3 (Oct 2023–Jan 2024), then a recovery back to 9. It moves opposite to session length (r = −0.81): users come back more often but stay shorter._

## Leading indicators

- **% of new trials that reach a first modeling output (a forecast built from their own imported data) in week 1:** It's the step right before the North Star and it sits at Activation, where Module 1 found 98% of trials stall; it responds within days of an onboarding change.
- **Average session length per trial user in the first 14 days:** Depth is the habit signal: Module 3 found churn rose as sessions got shorter (r = −0.37), and the weekly cash check is built to make each visit deeper, not just more frequent.

_____

## Lagging indicators

- **Trial-to-paid conversion rate:** It's the business outcome the strategy has to earn; at about 10 conversions a month it moves too rarely to experiment on directly, so we read it after the leading indicators move.
